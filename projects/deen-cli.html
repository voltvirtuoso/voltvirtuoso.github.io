deen-cli

A Git-style Islamic productivity companion for private prayer tracking, authenticated spiritual content, and local records—built for the terminal.

deen-cli is a terminal companion for keeping spiritual practice organised without turning it into a public dashboard. It combines prayer-time setup and tracking with Quran, hadith, and dua discovery in a straightforward, private workflow.

Working principle

Use familiar Git-style language for personal records: log meaningful actions, inspect status, and keep the data on the user’s own device.

Overview

The project began with a practical question: how can a person keep a small, useful spiritual record without needing another web account or a crowded mobile interface? The answer is a local Python CLI with a guided first-run setup, SQLite storage, and content services that are used only when the command needs them.

Prayer times are location-aware, personal activity can be recorded as concise commits, and reading modes make it possible to browse a surah, hadith collection, or dua category without flooding the terminal.

Key workflows

  • Prayer status and tracking — receives local prayer times, shows the next prayer, and records completed or makeup prayers.
  • Authenticated spiritual content — surfaces Quran, hadith, and dua content with source-aware reading commands and Arabic rendering support.
  • Git-style habit records — uses small commits and logs for Quran reading, salah, dhikr, and other personal practice.
  • Private local storage — maintains personal records in a local SQLite database rather than a hosted account.
  • Guided setup — offers deen init to configure a location and practical defaults before the first use.

Command design

The commands are intentionally short and readable. They separate discovery from complete reading, which makes a quick moment of inspiration different from opening a longer collection.

# First-time setup and daily status
deen init
deen status

# Log a personal practice
deen commit -t salah -m "Dhuhr"
deen log --days 30

# Read and discover source-aware content
deen read-surah --surah 1 --page 1
deen inspire --type hadith --mood stressed
