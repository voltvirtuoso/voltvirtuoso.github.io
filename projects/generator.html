Generator Auto On/Off

Automatic generator start-up within seconds of a grid outage, paired with a self-diagnostic indicator for common engine faults.

An automation project that starts a generator within seconds of a grid outage and surfaces a self-diagnostic fault indicator, so common engine faults are easier to spot without a manual check.

Overview

Manual generator switching is slow exactly when speed matters most — during an outage. This project automated the response: detect a loss of grid supply and start the generator within seconds, removing the delay and guesswork of a manual switch-over, while a self-diagnostic indicator flags common engine faults directly.

Key features

  • Automatic start on grid loss — the generator starts within seconds of detecting a supply failure.
  • Self-diagnostic fault indication — common engine faults are signalled directly, supporting faster troubleshooting.
  • Reduced manual intervention — the switch-over no longer depends on someone noticing the outage and acting quickly.
Operating goal

Continuity — automate the response to loss of supply while making common faults easier to identify, rather than leaving both jobs to a person reacting in the dark.

Tech stack

A microcontroller watches the grid-supply signal and drives the generator's start circuitry, with a diagnostic layer reading fault indicators from the engine and surfacing them without needing a manual panel check.

Outcome

The automated response closed the gap between an outage starting and the generator taking over, and the diagnostic indicator turned troubleshooting from an at-the-panel guessing exercise into something that could be checked at a glance.
