01

Build for the conditions outside the lab.

Reliability is not an afterthought: it is state persistence, clear fallbacks, tested signals, usable telemetry, and sensible control logic.

01 / SENSE

Read the physical system

Current transformers, temperature and humidity sensors, water-level sensors, image data, or field equipment records.

02 / DECIDE

Make the operating choice explicit

Priorities, thresholds, PID control, event logic, and safe restart behaviour keep a system understandable.

03 / CONNECT

Expose useful information

Firebase, MQTT, dashboards, PBX tooling, containerized services, and mobile interfaces turn raw signals into action.

04 / VERIFY

Respect the details

Asset checks, lab validation, debouncing, fault indicators, and direct observation all support better engineering decisions.
