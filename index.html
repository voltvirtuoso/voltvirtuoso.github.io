About

I'm an electrical engineer from Islamabad. At VELOSI I build asset registers for industrial sites: pulling equipment data from photos and documents, checking it against P&IDs, and ranking equipment by criticality.

Outside of work I build ESP32 and IoT projects, and I'm increasingly interested in running AI on small hardware. More about me
