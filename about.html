Introduction

I'm Haroon Raza, an Instrument Engineer with a BSc in Electrical Engineering from COMSATS University Islamabad (CGPA 3.73/4.00). Since March 2026 I've worked at VELOSI, where I build ISO-based asset registers, verify equipment against P&IDs, and run equipment criticality analysis.

Before that I did a traineeship at CMGL building ESP32 projects (a LoRa tracker, a water level controller, an IoT CNC machine), a VoIP internship at GOMARKHO, and a few shorter internships in firmware and computer vision. My final year project was a Smart Cart ML module, and I maintain two open-source tools, Pylex and deen-cli.

I'm a curious learner by default, I like taking things apart to understand them, and I welcome constructive criticism and open discussion as a normal part of getting a design right, not a threat to it. That same curiosity is pulling me toward integrating AI with hardware through embedded systems, and toward automation more broadly: I think most repetitive, predictable tasks are candidates for automating away, so that people can spend their attention on the work that actually needs a person.

I try to document what I build so whoever picks it up next, whether it's a PCB, a deployed service or an asset register, can follow it without asking me.
